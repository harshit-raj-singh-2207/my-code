FAQ

Questions people ask before enrolling.

If you don’t see your question here, you can always reach out using the contact form below.

Do I need prior marketing experience? ＋
No. We start from the fundamentals and move towards intermediate topics. Basic comfort with using a laptop and the internet is enough.
What tools will we use? ＋
We’ll use live dashboards from Google Analytics, Meta Ads Manager, Google Ads, and simple spreadsheets/Notion templates for planning content.
Will I get recordings? ＋
Yes. All sessions are recorded and available to you for a limited time after the cohort ends, along with key resources and templates.
Do you provide a certificate? ＋
Yes. Learners who complete the capstone project receive a digital certificate that can be shared on LinkedIn and portfolios.
Is job placement guaranteed? ＋
We do not guarantee jobs. However, we offer guidance, portfolio reviews, and interview preparation support to help you stand out.
Can I get a refund? ＋
Yes. You can request a full refund up to 48 hours after the first live session if you feel the workshop isn’t the right fit.
What if I miss a session? ＋
You can catch up using the recordings and resources, and post your questions in the community or weekly office hours.
Will this help if I’m a freelancer? ＋
Absolutely. We cover how to talk about your work, structure case studies, and communicate results — critical skills for freelancers.
