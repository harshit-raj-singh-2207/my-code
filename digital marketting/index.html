About

What this workshop gives you

This is a practical, developer-friendly Digital Marketing workshop built to help technically-minded people ship measurable campaigns. Expect code-friendly lessons: analytics with GA4, GTM snippets, automated reporting and A/B testing setup.

  • Hands-on campaigns — build and launch an actual ad & SEO plan
  • Quick-start analytics — GTM + GA4 integration and dashboards
  • Performance-first strategies — CRO, landing pages and funnels

Who should join?

Developers, product builders, content creators, growth interns — anyone who wants to run data-driven marketing with minimal dependence on agencies.
